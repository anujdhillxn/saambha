For alarms and reminders:
- Use `at` to schedule and `paplay` for sound:
  echo "DISPLAY=:1 paplay /usr/share/sounds/freedesktop/stereo/alarm-clock-elapsed.oga && DISPLAY=:1 zenity --info --text='<reminder text>'" | at <time>
- Examples: `at 7:30`, `at now + 10 minutes`, `at 14:00 tomorrow`
- To list pending alarms: atq
- To cancel an alarm: atrm <job_id>
